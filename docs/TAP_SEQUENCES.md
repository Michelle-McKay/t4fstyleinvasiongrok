# Tap sequences (reference)

Every click, in order, for each base building. This is design reference only: it is not shown in the game.

## Command Center

1. Tap the Command Center plot.
2. Tap an action tile (Requisition, Muster, Recall all, Shield, Relocate). Locked tiles show the level they open at.
3. Read Next level: every requirement shows a tick or a cross. Tap a cross row to jump to that building.
4. Tap Upgrade. If you are short, the button reads "cover N" and spends diamonds for the gap.
5. Watch the ring on the plot. Tap Rush (diamonds), Slip (a speed-up item) or Ask help (alliance).
6. A green bubble shows when it is done. Tap the plot to clear it and see the new look.

## Military Complex

1. Tap the Military Complex plot.
2. Tap Open Train.
3. Pick a class, then a tier. Locked tiers say what they need.
4. Set the batch with the steppers, or tap Max.
5. Tap Train. A ring shows on the complex.
6. When the bubble appears, the troops are in the garrison.

## Tech Institute

1. Tap the Tech Institute plot.
2. Tap Open Lab.
3. Pick a tree tab, then read the requirement line on a card.
4. Tap Research. One lab queue only.
5. Use Rush, Slip or Ask help on the ring if you are in a hurry.

## Depot

1. Tap the Depot plot.
2. Tap Open Med. The Depot tab lists your wounded.
3. Tap Heal all on a row. Tier 1 is instant, higher tiers take a timer.
4. Beds show used over capacity. Overflow dies, so heal early.

## Defense Center

1. Tap the Defense Center plot.
2. Tap Open Wall.
3. Pick a wall class and tier. Tiers unlock with Defense Center level.
4. Set the batch and tap Crew.
5. Crew cap is 40 per Defense Center level.

## Hall of War

1. Tap the Hall of War plot.
2. Tap Open Rally.
3. Pick a target, a wait, and extra slots if you have tokens.
4. Fill the column, tap Lead rally. Only you can cancel it.

## Radar Station

1. Tap the Radar Station plot.
2. At level 4 or more, tap Anti-Scout to block scouts and see who sent them.
3. Recon research raises scan depth, up to the station level.

## StoreHouse

1. Tap the StoreHouse plot.
2. Read the cap and the protected floor. Raids cannot take the floor.
3. Upgrade to raise both.

## Treasury

1. Tap the Treasury plot.
2. Each level adds Cash every hour. Upgrade to earn more.

## Prison

1. Tap the Prison plot.
2. Seals are spent to ransom a captured hero. Tap the seals button to buy five.

## Black Market

1. Tap the Black Market plot.
2. Tap Open Market.
3. Three offers a day. Refresh costs 15 diamonds.

## Rations (rural)

1. Tap the field or plant.
2. Tap Harvest to collect one minute of output now (2 minute cooldown).
3. Tap Root path to see which buildings this feeds and which Command Center levels need it.
4. Read Details: output, yield bonus, and how long until the store is full.
5. Tap Upgrade. Rural buildings have no prerequisites: they are the roots of every path.
6. Watch the ring, then tap the plot when the bubble shows.

## Empty plot (any building)

1. Tap an empty plot.
2. Tap Build on a building row.
3. A ring shows the build timer. Rush, Slip or Ask help to speed it.
4. Tap the plot again when the bubble shows.
